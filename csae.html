Shaheer Video Downloader Zero‑Tolerance Policy

Safety Standards Against Child Sexual Abuse & Exploitation (CSAE)

We operate a strict, zero‑tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any form of child exploitation. Our app must never be used to create, download, store, share, or facilitate access to such content. Violations are removed and reported without delay.

Scope

These standards apply to all features of Shaheer Video Downloader, including downloading, saving, sharing, and viewing media obtained via the app.

Prohibited Content

  • Any material that depicts or sexualizes minors (under 18 years of age), including edited, AI‑generated, or simulated content.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or sexual exploitation of minors in any form.
  • Links, instructions, or tools intended to locate, trade, or distribute CSAM.

Enforcement Actions

  • Immediate removal of known or suspected CSAE content accessible via the app.
  • Permanent bans for users who attempt to use the app for CSAE.
  • Reports to appropriate authorities, including the NCMEC CyberTipline (where applicable) and/or local law‑enforcement.
  • Cooperation with law enforcement consistent with applicable laws and due process.

Detection & Review

We investigate user reports promptly and prioritize child safety. If CSAE is suspected, we preserve minimal necessary information to support lawful reporting and investigations.

How to Report Suspected CSAE

  1. Contact us: support@shaheervideodownloader.app
  2. Include relevant details (URLs, timestamps, platform/source).
  3. Do not share or forward illegal content. Provide descriptions only.

External Reporting

You may also report directly to your local authority. If you are in the United States, use the NCMEC CyberTipline. In other regions, contact national hotlines or law‑enforcement agencies responsible for child protection.

User Responsibilities

  • Only download content you have a legal right to access.
  • Never search for, save, or share content that exploits minors.
  • Report suspected CSAE immediately via the channels above.

Privacy & Data Handling

We do not scan unrelated device data. For CSAE reports, we retain only what is necessary to fulfill legal reporting duties and protect users, consistent with our Privacy Policy.

Commitment to Continuous Improvement

We regularly review these standards and our moderation workflows to meet or exceed evolving best practices and legal requirements.

Important: If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number right away.
